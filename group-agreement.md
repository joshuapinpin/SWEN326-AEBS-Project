# Group Agreement

## Values / Norms / Expectations
- No judgement within the group project.
- Responsibility: members are responsible for their piece of work and the outcome of it.
- Accountability: members will take accountability for what is done.
- Respect amongst all members.
- Maintain a friendly environment within the team where we can ask each other for help and support one another.

## Meetings / Contribution
- Meetings will be held on call or in person at least once a week to discuss progress and what we are doing.
- The team has agreed on **Wednesday, 12pm – 1pm** as the meeting time.
- Each member will contribute at least **4 hours per week** toward the project and will update other members on their progress.
- If someone cannot attend a meeting, they must contribute in their own time and update the group through the agreed communication channels.

## Coding Environment
- Using IntelliJ as our IDE.
- Code is written in Java 21.
- Agreement on the use of AI tools to support development.
